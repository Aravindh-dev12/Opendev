# Escalation guide

Escalate by calling ask_human with: what you were doing, what blocked you, the options you see, and who should answer.

| Situation | Who | Operator action |
|---|---|---|
| Unknown or blocked vendor | Procurement | Do nothing in the ERP |
| Missing PO number | Requester | Do nothing in the ERP |
| Amount mismatch above 2 percent | Buyer | Enter invoice, put On Hold |
| Over limit invoice | Manager | Submit for manager approval |
| Duplicate invoice | Requester | Do nothing in the ERP |

Escalations never stop the rest of the batch. Continue with the remaining invoices.
